Understanding Fuzzy Logic in Medicine

Traditional vs Fuzzy Logic

Traditional: Blood sugar = 125 mg/dL → Either "High" or "Not High"

Fuzzy: Blood sugar = 125 mg/dL → 60% High, 30% Slightly High, 10% Normal

This better reflects clinical reality where boundaries are not sharp.

Risk Levels

  • Medium Risk (30-60%) - Consult physician
  • High Risk (60-100%) - Immediate attention needed
