About This System

What is Fuzzy Logic?

Fuzzy logic is a mathematical approach that handles the concept of partial truth. Unlike classical binary logic where a value must be true (1) or false (0), fuzzy logic allows for values between 0 and 1, representing degrees of membership in a set.

In medical decision support, this is particularly useful because clinical parameters don't have sharp boundaries. For example, a blood sugar level of 125 mg/dL is not simply "high" or "not high" - it has characteristics of both slightly elevated and high ranges.

How Our System Works

  1. Fuzzification: Convert crisp input values (blood sugar, BMI, etc.) into fuzzy membership values
  2. Rule Evaluation: Apply 25+ fuzzy rules based on medical knowledge to evaluate the inputs
  3. Aggregation: Combine the results of all rules
  4. Defuzzification: Convert the fuzzy output back into a crisp risk score (0-100%)
  5. Decision Support: Generate clinical recommendations based on the risk category

Input Parameters

Blood Sugar

Range: 0-600 mg/dL

Significance: High blood sugar is a primary indicator of diabetes risk

Normal Fasting: 70-100 mg/dL

Body Mass Index

Range: 10-60 kg/m²

Calculation: Weight (kg) / Height (m)²

Normal Range: 18.5-24.9 kg/m²

Age

Range: 0-120 years

Significance: Age is an important risk factor for diabetes

Risk Increases: Over 40 years

Blood Pressure

Range: 40-250 mmHg

Measured: Systolic pressure (top number)

Normal: Less than 120 mmHg

Risk Categories

Low Risk (0-30%)

Indicates a low probability of developing diabetes in the near future. Continue current lifestyle habits and maintain regular health check-ups.

Medium Risk (30-60%)

Indicates moderate risk. Consultation with a healthcare provider is recommended for further evaluation and preventive measures.

High Risk (60-100%)

Indicates significant risk. Immediate medical consultation is recommended for comprehensive evaluation and treatment planning.

Fuzzy Rules

Our system uses 25 fuzzy rules to evaluate diabetes risk. These rules are based on medical literature and expert knowledge:

Rule: IF Blood Sugar is Normal AND BMI is Normal AND Age is Young THEN Risk is Low

Logic: Young patients with normal parameters have low diabetes risk

Rule: IF Blood Sugar is High AND BMI is Obese THEN Risk is High

Logic: High blood sugar combined with obesity significantly increases risk

Rule: IF Blood Sugar is Very High THEN Risk is High

Logic: Very high blood sugar alone indicates significant risk

Rule: IF Blood Sugar is Slightly High AND Age is Old AND BP is High THEN Risk is Medium

Logic: Multiple elevated factors in older patients indicate medium risk

Membership Functions

We use triangular membership functions to represent fuzzy sets for each input variable:

  • Blood Sugar: Normal, Slightly High, High, Very High
  • BMI: Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese
  • Age: Young, Middle-aged, Old
  • Blood Pressure: Normal, Elevated, High

Each membership function has a smooth, gradual transition between categories, allowing for nuanced representation of clinical parameters.
